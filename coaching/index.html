What you’ll learn

The same steps I follow for every story, shaped around what you want to make.

  1. 1
    Find a story that feels trueShape romance and family drama ideas that fit African customs, including Nigerian and Ghanaian traditions around family, courtship and marriage.
  2. 2
    Plan your shotsTurn your story into scenes and a simple shot list before you generate anything.
  3. 3
    Write prompts that workDescribe people, places, light and emotion so the AI gives you the scene you pictured.
  4. 4
    Keep your characters consistentMake the same faces, outfits and settings carry from one scene to the next.
  5. 5
    Generate with AI toolsCreate photoreal video scenes and talking dialogue with the kind of tools I use.
  6. 6
    Edit like a finished filmPut it together with captions, your logo and titles.
  7. 7
    Post and shareGet your movie out on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.
